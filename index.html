About Kazigaon

Kazigaon is a peaceful and developing village located in Kokrajhar District, Assam (PIN - 783339). It lies in the Parbatjhora region and is well known for its natural beauty, friendly people and cultural diversity. People from different communities such as Rabha, Bodo, Garo and other tribes live together with unity and harmony. Hindus, Christians and Muslims celebrate their festivals together, making Kazigaon an example of communal harmony. One of the biggest attractions of Kazigaon is the beautiful Kazigaon Waterfall located at Dholongjhora. During the monsoon season, the waterfall becomes one of the most beautiful natural destinations in Lower Assam and attracts many visitors. Kazigaon is continuously developing with better education, health facilities, roads, sports infrastructure and eco-tourism projects. In the coming years it is expected to become one of the important tourism destinations in Assam.

🌊 Kazigaon Waterfall

The beautiful Kazigaon Waterfall is located at Dholongjhora, Kazigaon Part-5, Kokrajhar District, Assam. It is one of the hidden natural attractions of Lower Assam. The waterfall is surrounded by green forests, fresh air, hills and peaceful nature. During the monsoon season, the waterfall becomes more beautiful with powerful flowing water. Visitors come here for picnics, photography, nature walks and relaxation. Kazigaon Waterfall has great potential to become one of the most popular eco-tourism destinations in Assam.

🌿 Culture & Communities

Kazigaon is a land of cultural diversity where people from different communities live together peacefully. The major communities include Rabha, Bodo, Garo and several other indigenous tribes. People respect each other's traditions and celebrate festivals with unity, making Kazigaon a symbol of communal harmony. Traditional dance, music, local food, handloom and cultural programmes are an important part of village life.

🛕 Religion & Festivals

Kazigaon is home to people of different religions including Hinduism, Christianity and Islam. Every community celebrates its festivals with joy and respect. Major festivals include: ✅ Bwisagu ✅ Durga Puja ✅ Eid ✅ Christmas ✅ Local Tribal Festivals These celebrations strengthen friendship and brotherhood among the villagers.

🚧 Future Development

Kazigaon is developing rapidly in different sectors. New educational institutions, healthcare facilities, road connectivity and public infrastructure are helping improve the quality of life. The Kazigaon Waterfall has significant potential to become an eco-tourism destination that can attract visitors from different parts of Assam and neighbouring states. Sports facilities, skill development centres and youth activities are also expanding. Future plans for infrastructure and tourism development, including the Zero Mile area and other local projects, are expected to make Kazigaon an important destination for tourism, education and sustainable rural development.
